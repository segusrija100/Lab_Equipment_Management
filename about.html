✦ ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Helping college laboratories
work smarter, every day.

LabEase is a college laboratory equipment management system designed to make inventory tracking, equipment requests, issue records and returns easier to manage.

◎

Who we are

LabEase is an academic full-stack web project focused on a common college need: keeping laboratory equipment information organized and accessible to the people responsible for it.

⌖

Our vision

To support well-organized laboratories where equipment can be located, requested, issued and returned with clear records and less manual effort.

✧

Our approach

We bring inventory details and transaction records together through a simple interface backed by a relational MySQL database.

WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES

From inventory to return, in one workflow.

Administrators can maintain equipment details and user records. Students and faculty can have their equipment requests recorded. Issue and return activities update available stock so the inventory stays useful.
